Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Fairtab ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website fairtab.online. This page describes what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google solely to identify your account. We do not store your Google password.

When you create or participate in a bill, we store the on-chain wallet address associated with your account, bill details, and payment records on the Arc blockchain. This data is public by nature of the blockchain.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your Fairtab account and wallet
  • To display your name and picture within the app
  • To associate you with bills you create or join

We Do Not

  • Sell your personal information to third parties
  • Use your data for advertising
  • Store your private keys — your wallet is self-custodial via Circle

Third-Party Services

We use Circle for wallet infrastructure and Google OAuth for authentication. Please review their privacy policies for details on how they handle your data.

Data Retention

On-chain data is permanent by nature of the blockchain and cannot be deleted. Off-chain account data (name, email, profile picture) is retained as long as your account is active. You may request deletion by contacting us.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at privacy@fairtab.online.
