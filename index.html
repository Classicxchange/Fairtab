Split bills fairly.
Settle in USDC.

Fairtab is a bill splitter for friends, flatmates and teams, built on Arc, Circle's blockchain for stablecoins. Create a shared bill for rent, utilities, a subscription or a dinner, let everyone pay their share in USDC, and the payee is paid automatically once the bill is fully funded. No chasing, no awkward reminders, no one holding the money for you.

How it works
  • Funds held by a smart contract, not by Fairtab
  • Sign in with Google, no seed phrase
  • USDC is also the network fee

How it works

  1. 1

    Create a bill

    Name it, add who owes what, choose who gets paid and set a deadline.

  2. 2

    Everyone pays their share

    People accept the invite and pay their exact share in USDC, all at once or in parts.

  3. 3

    The payee is paid

    When the bill is fully funded, the smart contract pays the payee automatically.

  4. 4

    Not paid in time? Money back

    If the deadline passes or the group cancels, everyone can take their money back.

Everything in one place

Fair shares

Split by fixed amounts or by percentages, with up to 20 people on one bill.

Consent first

Nobody pays until they accept. Invitations expire after 24 hours if they are ignored.

Pay in parts

Each person can pay their share in installments before the deadline.

Protected by a contract

Money sits in a smart contract on Arc, not with Fairtab. A group vote can cancel a bill and refunds follow.

Recurring bills

Rent and subscriptions can repeat on a schedule so you do not set them up again.

Sign in with Google

No seed phrase to start. A wallet is created for your Google account and every payment needs your own PIN. Prefer MetaMask or OKX? Use that instead.

Send and receive USDC

Send USDC to any wallet on Arc, or share a link or QR code to get paid.

FairID names

Claim a short name like @tunde and let people pay you without a long wallet address.

Commit idle USDC

Put spare USDC into Morpho lending vaults on Arc and watch your earnings grow live. You can withdraw whenever the vault has funds free.

Reminders

A bell shows invitations waiting, bills due soon and who still owes you, with a ready-made message to nudge them.

Easy top-ups

Add USDC from your wallet in a few steps. On Arc, USDC also pays the network fee, so there is no second token to buy.

Your money stays yours

Fairtab never holds your funds and cannot freeze or recover them. Payments go to a smart contract on Arc that follows fixed rules everyone can read. There is no Fairtab fee for splitting a bill: you only pay the small network fee, in USDC. Commit, where you put idle USDC into a lending vault, is the one feature that may carry a small one-time fee, shown before you confirm.

Real money, so go gently

Fairtab runs on Arc Mainnet. USDC and fees are real, and transactions cannot be reversed. Start with a small bill. Earning yield through Commit is not guaranteed and you can lose money, so read the notes in the app before you use it.

How Fairtab uses Google sign-in

You can sign in with Google to identify your account. Fairtab receives your name, email address and profile picture from Google only to show them in the app. See the privacy policy for details.
