Split bills fairly.
Settle in USDC.

Fairtab is a bill splitter for friends, flatmates and teams, built on Arc, Circle's blockchain for stablecoins. Split bills, send USDC to one wallet or many, receive, swap, earn and ask an assistant, all from one wallet. Create a shared bill for rent, utilities, a subscription or a dinner, let everyone pay their share in USDC, and the payee is paid automatically once the bill is fully funded. No chasing, no awkward reminders, no one holding the money for you.

How it works
  • Funds held by a smart contract, not by Fairtab
  • Sign in with Google, no seed phrase
  • USDC is also the network fee

How it works

  1. 1

    Create a bill

    Name it, add who owes what, choose who gets paid and set a deadline.

  2. 2

    Everyone pays their share

    People accept the invite and pay their exact share in USDC, all at once or in parts.

  3. 3

    The payee is paid

    When the bill is fully funded, the smart contract pays the payee automatically.

  4. 4

    Not paid in time? Money back

    If the deadline passes or the group cancels, everyone can take their money back.

Bills

Split any bill. Get paid in full.

Rent, utilities, a subscription or a dinner. One person creates the bill, everyone pays their own share, and the payee is paid by the smart contract.

  • Split by fixed amounts or percentages, with up to 20 people on one bill.
  • Consent first: nobody pays until they accept, and invitations expire after 24 hours if ignored.
  • Pay your share all at once or in parts before the deadline.
  • Recurring bills repeat on a schedule, so rent and subscriptions are set up once.
  • If the deadline passes or the group votes to cancel, refunds follow.
  • A bell shows invitations waiting, bills due soon and who still owes you.

Send

Send USDC to one wallet, or to many at once.

Send USDC to any wallet on Arc, or switch to Bulk and pay a whole list in one go. Claim a FairID like @tunde so people can pay you without a long address.

  • Individual: paste a wallet address or type a FairID name, review it and confirm.
  • Bulk: paste a list or upload a CSV of wallets and amounts, up to 200 people, for payroll, rewards or airdrops.
  • Fairtab checks who can receive first. You confirm once for every 50 transfers, and every payment comes from your own wallet.
  • Paid everyone and need another round? Change just the amount and send the same list again.
  • On Arc, USDC also pays the network fee, so there is no second token to buy.

FairID

A short name instead of a long address.

Claim a name like @tunde and let people pay you without copying a long wallet address.

  • Pick 3 to 20 letters, numbers or underscores. Names are first come, first served.
  • Claiming costs a one-time 1 USDC, shown before you confirm.
  • People pay you by typing your @name on the Send screen.
  • A FairID is only a label for your wallet. It never holds money, and you can release it or move it to another wallet.

Commit

Put idle USDC to work.

Commit spare USDC to Morpho lending vaults on Arc and watch your earnings grow live, block by block.

  • Choose between the Steakhouse Prime USDC and Galaxy USDC vaults.
  • Withdraw whenever the vault has funds free.
  • Commit may carry a small one-time fee, shown before you confirm.

Earnings are not guaranteed and you can lose money. Read the notes in the app before you commit.

Veltra

Ask Veltra instead of filling forms.

Veltra is the built-in assistant. Describe what you want in plain words and it prepares the screen for you.

  • Answers questions about bills, sends, swaps, how Fairtab works and the Arc blockchain it runs on.
  • Prepares a bill draft, a send, a bulk send or a swap, then opens the normal screen.
  • Uses only the wallet addresses you type. It never adds your wallet on its own.
  • Veltra never signs or sends anything. You review and confirm every transaction.

Circle wallet

A wallet without a seed phrase.

  • Every payment needs your own PIN, so only you can move your money.
  • Fairtab never holds your funds and cannot freeze or recover them.

Your money stays yours

Fairtab never holds your funds and cannot freeze or recover them. Payments go to a smart contract on Arc that follows fixed rules everyone can read. There is no Fairtab fee for splitting a bill: you only pay the small network fee, in USDC. Commit, where you put idle USDC into a lending vault, is the one feature that may carry a small one-time fee, shown before you confirm.

Real money, so go gently

Fairtab runs on Arc Mainnet. USDC and fees are real, and transactions cannot be reversed. Start with a small bill. Earning yield through Commit is not guaranteed and you can lose money, so read the notes in the app before you use it.

How Fairtab uses Google sign-in

You can sign in with Google to identify your account. Fairtab receives your name, email address and profile picture from Google only to show them in the app. See the privacy policy for details.
