Fairtab is a household bill splitter on the Arc blockchain. Create a shared bill for rent, utilities or anything else, let everyone pay their share in USDC, and the payee is paid once the bill is fully funded.

Get started

Split with anyone

Create a bill, assign each person a fixed or percentage share, and send them a link.

Pay in USDC

Participants pay their exact share and the payee is paid automatically once the bill is fully funded.

Self-custodial

Funds are held by a smart contract on Arc, not by Fairtab. You own your wallet and your keys.

How Fairtab uses Google sign-in

You can sign in with Google to identify your account. Fairtab receives your name, email address and profile picture from Google only to show them in the app. See the privacy policy for details.
